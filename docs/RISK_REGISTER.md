# SENTINEL-X — Security & Operational Risk Register

| Risk ID | Category | Description | Likelihood | Impact | Severity | Mitigation Strategy | Owner | Review Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-01** | **Safety / Operational** | Autonomous action execution without human verification causes facility lockdown or operational outage. | Low | Critical | **HIGH** | Strict simulation-only boundary. System is architecturally prohibited from invoking physical or network actuator APIs. | Cybersecurity Architect | Mitigated |
| **RSK-02** | **Data Integrity** | TTL indexes configured on MongoDB Atlas accidentally prune historical evidence required for timeline replay. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Explicit database validation rule: zero TTL indexes allowed on intelligence collections. Automated test validates index specs. | DevSecOps Engineer | Verified |
| **RSK-03** | **Model Reliability** | Opaque heuristic models produce hallucinations or unwarranted confidence percentages. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | 100% deterministic intelligence core. Bounded risk velocity and rule-based state transitions. Unmeasured metrics marked `TO BE VALIDATED`. | AI/ML Engineer | Mitigated |
| **RSK-04** | **Privacy & Compliance** | Exposure of employee biometric/badge IDs and CCTV detection metadata violates GDPR / privacy policies. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Role-based data pseudonymization, field-level masking for Viewer roles, and explicit data classification documented in `PRIVACY.md`. | Privacy Officer | In Progress |
| **RSK-05** | **Availability** | Event flood / burst attack overwhelms correlation buffer and degrades responsiveness. | High | Medium | **MEDIUM** | Asynchronous batch ingestion, sliding window expiration for correlation buffer, and performance benchmark thresholds. | Reliability Engineer | Mitigated |
| **RSK-06** | **Supply Chain** | Third-party dependencies (npm/pip) introduce vulnerabilities. | Medium | High | **MEDIUM** | Strict dependency pinning (`requirements.txt`, `package-lock.json`), zero unapproved heavy infrastructure, automated audit tools. | DevSecOps Engineer | Continuous |
| **RSK-07** | **Feedback Poisoning** | Malicious or erroneous operator feedback corrupts the baseline or model retraining datasets. | Low | High | **MEDIUM** | Operator feedback is logged to audit trail and isolated from live production state machine; models require offline validation before deployment. | Threat Intel Engineer | Mitigated |
| **RSK-08** | **Cold-Start Error** | New entity or newly deployed sensor triggers false-positive escalation due to absence of history. | High | Medium | **MEDIUM** | Cold-start state tracking with damped confidence scores until minimum observation threshold ($N \ge 10$) is satisfied. | AI/ML Engineer | Mitigated |
